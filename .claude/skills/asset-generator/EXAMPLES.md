## Quick Tips

1. **Copy the exact hex codes** shown in the examples to your brand colors
2. **Keep dimensions consistent** — don't shrink them after generation, test at actual size
3. **Generate sets together** — request multiple related assets in one generation for consistency
4. **Test on your site** — download and place the generated asset in context before finalizing
5. **Name your assets** — Use the asset name (e.g., "hero-tech-collaboration") in your brief

---

## How to Use These Examples

1. Find an example matching what you need
2. Customize the hex colors to match your brand palette
3. Adjust dimensions if needed (but keep aspect ratios sensible)
4. Copy the entire brief block
5. Paste into the asset-generator skill request
6. Generate!

Example customization:

**Original:**
```
Palette:
  - Background: #FAFAFA (light gray)
  - Primary: #2D3436 (dark)
  - Accent: #4A90E2 (blue)
```

**Customized to your brand:**
```
Palette:
  - Background: #FFFFFF (white)
  - Primary: #1A1A1A (your brand dark)
  - Accent: #6C2E92 (your brand purple)
```

That's it! The brief will now generate assets in your brand colors.
