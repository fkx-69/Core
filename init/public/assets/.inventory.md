## Asset Style Guide

### Minimalist
- Clean geometric forms
- 1.5-2px strokes
- Limited palette (2-3 colors max)
- Line art or flat fills

### Editorial
- Hand-drawn quality
- Warm/cool neutrals
- Scene-based
- Character forward when appropriate

### Technical
- Clean sans-serif if text
- Dashboard elements
- Isometric or flat perspective
- Professional scheme

### Playful
- Rounded forms
- Expressive characters
- Bold accents
- Brand personality
